# How to Add Product Images

## Your uploaded images need to be placed in the public/images folder

To make your product images display properly, follow these steps:

### 1. Add Images to the Correct Folder

Place your images in: `public/images/`

The images you need to add are:
- `calvin-hoodie.jpg`
- `burberry-sweater.jpg`
- `orange-notebook.jpg`
- `notebook-collection.jpg`
- `keychain-silver.jpg`
- `lbs-white-pen.jpg`
- `lbs-black-pen.jpg`

### 2. Current Setup

The product database is ready to display these images once you add them to the public/images folder.

Current product image paths configured:
- **Calvin Hoodie**: `/images/calvin-hoodie.jpg`
- **Burberry Sweater**: `/images/burberry-sweater.jpg`
- **Orange Notebook**: `/images/orange-notebook.jpg`
- **Notebook Collection**: `/images/notebook-collection.jpg`
- **Keychain Silver**: `/images/keychain-silver.jpg`
- **LBS White Pen**: `/images/lbs-white-pen.jpg`
- **LBS Black Pen**: `/images/lbs-black-pen.jpg`

### 3. Recommended Image Format

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 500x500px (optimal for product cards)
- **Quality**: High quality for best display

### 4. How to Upload

1. Open your file explorer
2. Navigate to: `D:\e-commerce-website-design\public\images\`
3. Paste your product images there with the exact filenames listed above
4. Restart your development server

Once you add the images, they will automatically display on your website!
